    What net 30 means

    Net 30 means the full amount is due 30 calendar days after the invoice date. Weekends and holidays count, so an invoice dated January 31 is due March 2 (2026). Net 15, net 45 and the others work the same way with a different number of days. "Due on receipt" means the due date is the invoice date.

    Some invoices count from a different starting point, such as the day the customer receives the work. Check your own terms and your customer agreement, because what applies to you can differ. This is not legal advice.

    When the due date lands on a Saturday or Sunday, this calculator does not move it. A customer may pay on the next working day, so the reminder before the due date matters more.
