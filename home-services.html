Straight answers

Do I have to change my phone number or phone company?

No — your number and provider stay exactly as they are. Unanswered calls quietly forward to a dedicated texting line set up for your business, and that's the number the text-back comes from, with your business name on it. Answering calls works exactly like today; whoever handles texts uses one simple inbox app.

We already use Jobber / Housecall Pro / ServiceTitan.

Good — keep it. We build around the tools you already run, connecting them so leads and jobs flow without anyone retyping things. We're not here to sell you new software.

What if a customer replies to the text?

You (or your office) see it and reply from a simple app — or we set up automatic answers for the common questions ("do you service my area?", "are you open Saturday?") and hand off to a human the moment it matters.

How fast is setup?

Missed-Call Rescue: about a week of setup on our side, then US carriers approve your business for texting — typically one to two weeks, and nobody can rush them. We file that on day one, and your monthly fee doesn't start until you're live. Bigger builds get a written timeline before you pay anything.

What happens if I cancel?

You keep everything. The systems are built in accounts you own, documented, with a video walkthrough — they keep running as long as you keep those accounts. No hostages.
