Sub-processors

Last updated: November 15, 2026

What this page is

A sub-processor is a company that handles data for Steadywork so that we can run the service. This page lists all of them.

The list

Company What it does for us What data it handles Where
Anthropic AI processing, through its Claude API. The content needed to produce a result: what you say in the interview, the contents of your files, data read from a connected system, and messages from visitors to your website chat. United States
Render Hosts the app and its database. Everything stored in your account. United States
Stripe Handles payments. Your card details, which go to Stripe and not to us, and your payment history. US company
Resend Sends email from the app. Receives the reports you email to your private address. Your email address, the emails the app sends you, and the emails and files you send to your private address. US company
Twilio Sends and receives text messages. Used only for the separate Missed-Call Rescue text-back service. Callers' phone numbers and the text messages. US company
GitHub Hosts this website and the app's source code. Standard technical logs from visits to this website, such as IP addresses. There is no customer data in the code. US company
cal.com Booking calls from this website. What you enter when you book a call, such as your name, email address and notes. US company

"US company" means the company is based in the United States. Each company has its own privacy policy, which says where it handles data.

If you use your own Anthropic API key, Anthropic handles your AI use under your own agreement with Anthropic.

Systems you connect

You can choose to connect QuickBooks Online (Intuit), Microsoft 365, Google, or Smartsheet. Those are your own providers. You have your own account and agreement with each one. They are not Steadywork's sub-processors.

How changes are announced

This page is updated when the list changes. If you have an account, we email you before a new sub-processor handles your data.
