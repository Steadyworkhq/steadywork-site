How we protect your data

Last updated: November 15, 2026

The main points

  • This page lists the security measures that are in place today.
  • It also lists what we do not have yet. We have no security certification and no outside penetration test.
  • You can download your data, delete your account, and disconnect any system yourself.
  • To report a security problem, email alex@steadyworkhq.com.

What is in place

Signing in

  • All connections use HTTPS.
  • Passwords are stored hashed. We do not store the password itself.
  • Sign-in attempts are limited.
  • Two-step sign-in is available, with an authenticator app and recovery codes. It is optional.
  • You can sign out all your other devices.

Keeping accounts apart

  • Each account's data is separated from every other account's by rules in the database.

Stored access and keys

  • Stored access to your connected systems is encrypted.
  • A stored Anthropic API key is encrypted.
  • The encryption key is held outside the database. There is one for each account.

Programs the AI writes

  • They run in a sealed environment.
  • They have no network access and no file access.

Record of security events

  • The app records sign-ins, failed attempts, password resets, and connections added or removed.
  • You can read this record in your account.

Checks on our code and site

  • Every change is checked by automated tests, dependency checks, code scanning, and a scan for leaked secrets.
  • The live site is scanned from the outside every week.

AI processing is done by Anthropic. Files uploaded to Anthropic for a run are deleted after the run. The Privacy Policy says more. The companies that handle data for us are on the Sub-processors page.

What you control

  • Download your data. Your details, your interviews, your instructions and rules, your drafts, the names of your connections and reports, and the action log.
  • Delete your account. You can delete your account, and everything in it, yourself.
  • Disconnect a system. You can disconnect any connected system at any time. The Privacy Policy says what is deleted when you do.
  • Two-step sign-in. You can switch it on or off.

What we do not have yet

Steadywork is a one-person business. These things are not in place:

  • No security certification. We do not have SOC 2 or ISO 27001.
  • No penetration test by an outside firm.
  • No uptime guarantee.
  • No bug bounty.

No system is perfectly secure, and we can't promise that ours is.

Reporting a security problem

If you find a security problem, or think someone else has used your account, email alex@steadyworkhq.com. Say what you found and how to see it.

If there is an incident

If a security incident affects your data, we will tell you without undue delay, and as the law requires, including Utah law (Utah Code § 13-44).
