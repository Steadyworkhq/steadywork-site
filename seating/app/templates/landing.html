Questions

What file can I upload?

CSV or Excel (.xlsx) with a name column and a table column — or copy and paste straight from a spreadsheet, Google Sheets, or a list like “Ana López - 4”. First and last names in separate columns work too. You'll confirm which column is which.

Who can see the QR page?

Anyone who has the link (for example, by scanning the sign). The page doesn't list your guests; a visitor has to type a guest's full name to see that guest's table. It's hidden from search engines. Your editing page is separate and private.

How long does the guest page stay online?

Until {{ retention_days }} days after your event date. Then the page and your guest list are deleted automatically. You can delete everything sooner from your dashboard.

What do you do with my guest list?

Only what's needed to make your files and run your seat finder. We ask for names and tables only — no addresses, meal choices or other details. Your list is not sent to any AI service. See privacy.

Can I fix a mistake after I pay?

Yes. Edit names and tables any time before your event and download fresh files. Already-printed copies won't change, but the QR page updates immediately.

Refunds?

If the files have a defect we can't fix, we refund you. See terms.
