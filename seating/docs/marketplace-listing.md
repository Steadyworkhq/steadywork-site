# Proposed Etsy listing (draft, not published)

Based on the rules summarised in `research.md` §1. The policy wording comes from search snippets, so check the live policy pages before publishing.

## Why this differs from the website offer

Etsy's Off-Platform Transactions Policy prohibits directing members off Etsy, including "using a QR code to direct members off Etsy". The Seller Policy restricts sharing external URLs. Services and software or subscription access are generally prohibited.

So the Etsy version is **made-to-order personalised digital files only**:

- Delivered through Etsy.
- Guest list collected through Etsy Messages or the personalisation box.
- Corrections handled in Etsy Messages.
- No external link, account, management page, upsell or off-Etsy QR page.

A QR guest page may be added later **only if Etsy Support confirms in writing** that a free, view-only guest lookup page we host is allowed. Until then, leave the QR sign out of the Etsy bundle.
