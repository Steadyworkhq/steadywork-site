Drafts, approval and your responsibility

The app interviews you about your work, produces a ranked list of tasks, and runs automations. An automation reads your data and prepares drafts. A draft can be an email, a document, or a proposed entry in a system you connected. The app also shows a dashboard. Any time-saved figure on it is an estimate based on your own figure, not a measurement.

By default, the app is designed to wait for your approval of a draft before anything is sent or changed in your systems. When you approve an email, you choose whether it is sent from your own mailbox, placed in your Drafts folder, or left for you to send.

You can set an individual automation to act without waiting for your approval. If you do, it sends or enters what it prepares on its own, within the limits you set. The app is designed to email you a list after each run. You are responsible for everything it sends or enters, exactly as if you had approved each item. You can switch this setting off.

An automation can also run as a fixed program with no AI. You decide whether to use one. You are responsible for what it prepares, sends or enters, in the same way as for any other automation.

AI output can be wrong. It can misread your data or say something you would not say. You must review each draft before you approve it. You are responsible for everything you approve, including the messages that go out and the entries that are made.

Entries in your systems

Every entry is made at your direction. Check the list before you confirm. You are responsible for the entries that are made. Steadywork is not responsible for errors in your systems that result from them.
