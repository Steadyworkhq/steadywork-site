What the app does

The app does three things:

  • It interviews you about your work. The interview is a chat with an AI.
  • It produces a ranked list of tasks worth automating.
  • It runs automations. An automation reads your data and prepares drafts. A draft can be an email, a document, or a proposed entry in a system you have connected, such as a bill in your accounting system or a row in a spreadsheet.

Approval and your responsibility

By default, nothing is sent, and nothing is changed in your systems, unless you approve that specific draft.

You can set an individual automation to act without waiting for your approval. If you do, it sends or enters what it prepares on its own, within the limits you set, and emails you a list after each run. You are responsible for everything it sends or enters this way, exactly as if you had approved each item. You can switch this off at any time.

When you approve an email, you choose what happens to it. It can be sent from your own mailbox. It can be placed in your Drafts folder. Or you can send it yourself. Each action is logged.

AI output can be wrong. It can misread your data or say something you would not say. Each draft is labelled either "Written by AI" or as made by fixed steps or a fixed program with no AI. Read each draft before you approve it. You are responsible for what you approve, including the messages that go out and the entries that are made.

A credit is a measure of AI use. Each plan includes a monthly allowance of credits.
