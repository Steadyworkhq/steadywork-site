Accessibility

Last reviewed August 9, 2026

Our commitment

We want everyone to be able to use this site, whatever tools they browse with. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA.

What we've built it to do

The site uses semantic headings and landmarks, works without a mouse — every control, including the missed-call calculator's sliders and the FAQ sections, is keyboard-operable — carries text alternatives where images convey information, avoids auto-playing media and motion entirely, and keeps text at contrast levels meant to stay readable.

Where it might fall short

Steadywork is a small business and this site is maintained by hand. We review it against WCAG when it changes, but we may miss things — and we'd rather hear about a problem than not.

Tell us

If any part of this site is hard to use with your setup — screen reader, keyboard, magnification, anything — email alex@steadyworkhq.com and tell us what happened and what browser or assistive technology you were using. We'll fix what we can promptly, and if you need the information on this site in another format, we'll simply send it to you.
