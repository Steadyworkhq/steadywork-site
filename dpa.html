When this applies

"You" means the business that holds the Steadywork account. "We" means Steadywork, a sole-proprietor business in Ogden, Utah.

"Your data" means the personal information we handle for you in the app. That includes information about your customers, your suppliers, your staff, and visitors to your website chat.

This addendum is intended to meet the obligations of a service provider or processor under US state privacy laws. It does not include the European Union's Standard Contractual Clauses. It is not written for data that is covered by the laws of the EU or the UK.

This addendum does not cover the information we hold about you as our customer, such as your sign-in email and your billing history. The Privacy Policy covers that.

Roles

You are the controller, or the "business". You decide what data goes into the app and what the app does with it.

Steadywork is the processor, or the "service provider". We handle your data for you, to run the app.

You are responsible for having the right to use your data in the app, and for telling the people it is about. If you use the website chat, that includes telling your visitors how their data is used, in your own privacy notice.

What is processed, why, and for how long

What

The personal information in what you put into the app or connect to it: your interviews, files you upload or email in, data read from systems you connect, your instructions and rules, drafts, and the action log. If you use the website chat: your visitors' messages, and any name, email address or phone number they give.

Who it is about

People who appear in your data. For example your customers, suppliers, staff, team members, and visitors to your website.

Why

To provide the service you set up: the interview, the ranked list, automations and their drafts, entries into your connected systems, the website chat, and the dashboard. Also to keep the service secure, fix problems, and give you support.

For how long

While your account is open. After that, as set out under Deletion or return at the end.

Your instructions

We process your data only on your instructions. Your instructions are the Terms, this addendum, and the way you use the app: what you upload, what you connect, what you set up, and what you approve.

We may also process your data where the law requires it.

What we do not do with your data

  • We do not sell it.
  • We do not share it for advertising.
  • We do not use it for our own purposes outside providing the service to you.
  • We do not use it to train or develop AI models, and we do not allow anyone else to. AI processing is done by Anthropic. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, your data is not used to train its models.

Confidentiality

We keep your data confidential. Steadywork is one person today. That person looks at your data only for the reasons in the Privacy Policy. Anyone who works for Steadywork in future will be bound to keep your data confidential before they can reach it.

Security

We keep the security measures listed on the Security page. That page also says what we do not have yet.

Sub-processors

We use other companies to help run the service. They are listed on the Sub-processors page. You agree to our use of them.

  • We update that page when the list changes.
  • If you have an account, we email you before a new sub-processor handles your data. If you do not agree to the change, you can stop using the service and delete your account.
  • We have a written agreement with each sub-processor. It passes on the same obligations for your data that we take on here.

Systems you choose to connect, such as QuickBooks Online, Microsoft 365, Google or Smartsheet, are your own providers. They are not our sub-processors.

Requests from individuals

A person may ask to see, correct or delete information about them. If such a request comes to us about your data, we pass it to you.

We help you answer these requests. The app lets you download your data and delete it yourself. If you need more help, email us.

Security incidents

If a security incident affects your data, we will tell you without undue delay, and as the law requires. We will tell you what we know about what happened and what data was affected, so that you can meet your own duties.

Deletion or return at the end

  • Return. You can download your data from your account at any time: your details, your interviews, your instructions and rules, your drafts, the names of your connections and reports, and the action log.
  • Deletion by you. You can delete your account yourself. That deletes the account and everything in it.
  • Deletion on request. If you ask us to delete your data, we do it within 30 days.
  • Disconnected systems. When you disconnect a system, stored copies of data read from it are deleted at once. Drafts made from that data are removed within 30 days.
  • If a paid plan ends. If your plan ends and you keep your account, your data is kept until you delete it or ask us to.
  • Backups. Backups are kept by our hosting provider on its own schedule, and they expire on that schedule. Deleted data can remain in a backup until that backup expires.

We may keep billing records where the law requires us to.

Information and audits

Steadywork is a small business. We cannot host on-site audits. Instead, we answer reasonable written requests for the information you need to check that we are keeping to this addendum. Email your request to alex@steadyworkhq.com.

Where data is processed

We process your data in the United States. The app and its database are hosted in the United States, and AI processing is done in the United States.

If this addendum changes, we'll post the new version here with a new date. If you have an account and the change matters to you, we will also email you.
