Automation for bookkeepers

Look after your clients' office busywork from one login.

Your clients ask you to chase their invoices, send their reminders and pull their weekly numbers together. Steadywork does that work as drafts: invoice emails, payment reminders, follow-ups, reports. Nothing goes out until someone approves it.

It does not replace the bookkeeping software you use. It takes the chores around it, the ones that eat your afternoons: chasing overdue invoices, reminders QuickBooks cannot tailor, and the weekly numbers each client wants. The guides explain the manual way for each.

One screen for all your clients

  • See every client at a glance: drafts waiting, automations, anything that needs attention.
  • Open a client and set up or adjust their automations from your own login, without their password.
  • Start them from ready-made automations, including a pack for contractors: lien waivers, insurance certificates, change orders, progress billing and retainage.

Your clients stay in charge

  • Each business gives you access from its own account, and chooses whether you may also approve and send drafts.
  • Billing, security and who may help stay with them.
  • Everything you do in their account is in their activity record, with your name.
  • If a client uses two-step sign-in, you need it too.
  • They can remove your access at any time, and it ends at once.

Bringing clients in

Invite a client by email, or share your own link. A business that signs up through it is asked whether you may help them. Nothing is shared until they say yes.
