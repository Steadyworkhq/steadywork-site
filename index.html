Straight answers

Do we have to buy new software?

Almost never. We build around what you already run — QuickBooks, Jobber, your phone system, your calendar — and connect it so information stops being retyped. If something genuinely needs a new tool, it'll be in the audit report with the monthly cost next to it, and you decide.

Is our data safe?

Yes, and we put it in writing. Client work runs only on business-tier AI services that don't train on your data, credentials are exchanged through an encrypted password manager (never email), and every system we build lives in accounts you own.

What does it cost?

The audit is $750 for small teams, $1,500 for ops-heavy companies — credited toward your build either way. Starter automations run from $199/mo fully managed. Bigger builds get a fixed written quote priced against what they return — no hourly meters, no surprises. Everything is cancel-anytime.

How fast?

Starter automations: about a week. Bigger builds like an auto-scheduler: a written timeline before you pay, typically a few weeks.

What happens if we cancel?

You keep everything — the systems, the documentation, the video walkthroughs. It all keeps working. No hostages.
