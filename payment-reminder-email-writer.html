When to send each one

Send the first reminder a few days after the due date. It assumes the invoice slipped through, so it stays light.

Send the second if the first got no answer, about a week later. It asks for a date or the reason for the delay. Send the final after that, when you need a firm answer. It sets a date a week out and asks for a reply. It makes no threats and mentions no fees or interest: those depend on your contract and your state, so this tool leaves them out.
