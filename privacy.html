Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

The main points

  • We use your data to run the app for you. We do not sell personal data.
  • AI work is done by Anthropic. Your content is sent to Anthropic to produce results. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, it is not used to train Anthropic's models.
  • The app sets one cookie to keep you signed in, and a short-lived one during two-step sign-in. There are no advertising or analytics cookies.
  • You can disconnect a connected system, download your data, and delete your account yourself, at any time.
  • If you put our chat on your website, we store what your visitors write so you can read it and follow up.

Who we are

Steadywork is a sole-proprietor business in Ogden, Utah. This policy covers the Steadywork app and this website, steadyworkhq.com. You can reach us at alex@steadyworkhq.com.

What the app collects

  • Account details. Your email address, your password (stored only in hashed form), and any name or business name you give us. If you switch on two-step sign-in, we also store what is needed to check your codes.
  • Your interview. What you tell the AI in the interview chat, the write-up made from it, and your ranked list of tasks.
  • Files you send in. Files you upload, and files you email to the private address we give you.
  • Data from connected systems. If you connect QuickBooks Online, Microsoft 365 (Outlook), Google (Gmail and Sheets), or Smartsheet, the app reads the data an automation needs from that system.
  • Automations and drafts. The instructions and rules you give, the drafts the app prepares, what you approve, and a log of each action.
  • Website chat. If you put our chat on your website: the facts you write for it, your visitors' messages, and any name, email address or phone number a visitor gives. See Website chat.
  • Dashboard numbers. What your automations handled, your own figure for how long a task takes, and numbers tracked from your data on each run.
  • Security events. A record of sign-ins, failed attempts, password resets, and connections added or removed. You can read this record in your account.
  • Connection details. The access tokens that let the app reach a system you connected. If you choose to use your own Anthropic API key, we store that key too.
  • Billing details. Your plan, your credit use, and your payment history. Payments are handled by Stripe.
  • Technical data. Standard server logs, such as IP addresses and the time of a request.
  • Messages to us. Anything you send us by email.

What this website collects

Almost nothing. The website is a static set of pages, separate from the app.

Server logs. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub, Inc. serves the pages and may log standard technical data like IP addresses, under its own privacy policy. We don't see or manage those logs.

Bookings. If you book a call, that happens through cal.com, a scheduling service we don't run. Whatever you enter there, such as your name, email, and notes, goes to cal.com and then to our calendar and email.

Email. If you email alex@steadyworkhq.com, we receive that email.

The website sets no cookies and runs no analytics. The calculator on the home-services page keeps nothing. It does math on the numbers you enter.

What we do with it

We use your data to:

  • run the interview, the ranked list, and your automations;
  • carry out what you approve, such as sending an email from your mailbox;
  • answer visitors in your website chat, if you set one up;
  • show your dashboard;
  • keep your account working, including sign-in and password reset;
  • bill you;
  • give you support, and prepare an audit or report if you ask for one;
  • reply to you and schedule calls;
  • keep the app secure and fix problems.

We never sell your information, and we never share it for marketing.

AI processing

The AI work in the app is done by Anthropic, through its Claude API. To produce an interview, a ranked list, or a draft, the app sends the content it needs to Anthropic. That can include what you said in the interview, the contents of your files, data read from a connected system, and messages from visitors to your website chat.

Under Anthropic's commercial terms, data sent through its API is not used to train its models.

Some automations upload a file to Anthropic for a run. Those files are deleted after the run.

If you use your own Anthropic API key, the same content is sent to Anthropic under your key. Your agreement with Anthropic then applies to that use, and Anthropic bills you for it. We store your key encrypted.

Some automations run as fixed steps or a fixed program, with no AI. Each draft is labelled so you can tell which kind made it.

Companies that handle data for us

These companies handle data so that we can run the app. Each has its own privacy policy. We share your data with no one else, unless the law requires it.

Anthropic

AI processing, in the United States. Receives the content needed to produce your results.

Render

Hosts the app and its database, in the United States.

Stripe

Handles payments. Your card details go to Stripe, not to us.

Resend

Delivers email the app sends, such as password resets and notices. Receives the files you email to your private address.

Twilio

Sends and receives text messages. Used only for the Missed-Call Rescue text-back service.

GitHub and cal.com

GitHub hosts this website and the app's source code. There is no customer data in the code. cal.com handles call bookings. See the GitHub privacy statement and the cal.com privacy policy.

The full list, with what data each company handles, is on the Sub-processors page. That page is updated when the list changes. If you have an account, we email you before a new company handles your data.

Systems you choose to connect, such as QuickBooks or Google, are your own providers. They are not on this list.

Connected systems

Connecting a system is optional. You can connect QuickBooks Online (Intuit), Microsoft 365 (Outlook), Google (Gmail and Sheets), or Smartsheet. You sign in on that company's own page, so we never see your password for it. The app asks for each permission separately, when it is first needed.

A connection is read-only unless you grant a permission that lets the app write, such as sending email from your mailbox. Even then, nothing is sent or changed unless you approve that specific draft, or you have set that automation to act without waiting for approval.

Access tokens are stored encrypted, with a key that is separate for each account.

You can disconnect a system at any time. When you do:

  • the app can no longer reach that system;
  • we cancel the access with that company, where the company supports this (Google and Intuit do);
  • we delete stored copies of data read from that system at once;
  • we remove drafts made from that system's data within 30 days.

Google accounts

If you connect a Google account, the app asks only for what the feature you are using needs, one permission at a time:

  • your email address, to show which account is connected;
  • permission to send email from your Gmail address, used only for emails you have approved, or that an automation you have set to act without approval sends;
  • permission to read, or to add a row to, a Google Sheet you name.

The app cannot read your email, file drafts in Gmail, or see your other files.

Steadywork's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • We use Google user data only to provide the features you see in the app.
  • To prepare a draft, the rows an automation reads from your sheet are sent to Anthropic, which processes them to produce the result. We do this only for that purpose and with your agreement, which the app asks for before you connect.
  • We do not use Google user data, and we do not let anyone else use it, to develop, improve or train general AI or machine-learning models.
  • We do not sell it or use it for advertising.
  • People at Steadywork do not read it, unless you ask us for help with it, it is needed for security, or the law requires it.

You can disconnect Google at any time under Connections. We then delete the access we hold and cancel it with Google. You can also delete your whole account, and everything in it, under Security and your data.

QuickBooks

If you connect QuickBooks Online, the app reads the data an automation needs. It can also prepare entries, such as a supplier bill. An entry is made only after you confirm it on a screen that lists exactly what will be created, unless you have set that automation to act without approval.

For data the app reads from QuickBooks:

  • We use it only to provide the features you set up.
  • To prepare a draft, it is sent to Anthropic, which processes it to produce the result.
  • We do not use it, and we do not allow anyone else to use it, to train or develop AI models.
  • We do not sell it. We do not share it, except with the companies needed to provide the service. They are listed on the Sub-processors page.
  • We delete it within 30 days of you disconnecting QuickBooks or closing your account.

You can disconnect QuickBooks at any time under Connections. We then cancel the access with Intuit and delete the access we hold. Stored copies of data read from QuickBooks are deleted at once. Drafts made from that data are removed within 30 days.

Website chat

You can put a Steadywork chat on your own website. It answers your visitors automatically, using only facts you wrote for it. You do not check each answer before it goes. Visitors are told the chat is automated.

What is stored. Your visitors' messages, and any name, email address or phone number they give. We store these so you can read them and follow up. When the chat cannot answer, it asks the visitor for contact details and creates a follow-up draft for you.

Where it goes. Visitors' messages are sent to Anthropic to produce the answer. The other companies on the Sub-processors page handle this data in the same way as the rest of your account.

Who is responsible. For your visitors' information, we act for you. You decide to run the chat, and you are responsible for telling your visitors how their data is used, in your own privacy notice.

If you are a visitor. If you used a chat on a business's website, that business holds your messages. Ask that business about them. If you write to us, we will pass your request to that business.

Dashboard

The dashboard shows what your automations handled, and numbers tracked from your data on each run. It also shows an estimate of time saved. The estimate is based on your own figure for how long the task takes.

A team owner sees the same for all of the team's automations. The dashboard does not track what people do outside the automations.

Teams

If you join a team with a team code, the team owner can read your interview write-up and the team's ranked list. You are told this when you join. If you don't want the owner to read something, don't put it in the interview.

The team owner can also:

  • see all of the team's automations, and the dashboard for them;
  • assign your automation to another member to cover. The cover person receives and approves its drafts, so they see the data in those drafts. What they approve can go out through your mailbox or theirs. The owner chooses which;
  • transfer an automation permanently to another member, with its rules, drafts and history.

Whoever takes on an automation is shown a handover page. It is built from the automation's record.

Who at Steadywork can see your data

Steadywork is one person. That person may look at your account data:

  • to give you support;
  • to prepare an audit or a report, if you ask for one;
  • to help with a broken automation. If you use "Fix it" and repeated repairs fail, we are alerted, and a person may look at that automation and its recent errors;
  • where it is needed for security, or the law requires it.

We don't look at it for any other reason.

Other people's information in your data

Your files and systems may hold information about other people, such as your customers' names, email addresses, and what they owe. We handle that information only to run the app for you, on your instructions. You are responsible for having the right to use it this way. If one of those people contacts us about their information, we will pass the request to you.

Cookies

The app sets a cookie called sw_session. It keeps you signed in. The app cannot work without it.

If you use two-step sign-in, the app also sets a short-lived cookie called sw_step while you sign in.

There are no advertising cookies and no analytics cookies.

This website sets no cookies.

Selling, tracking and opt-out signals

We don't sell personal data. We don't use ad tech or data brokers.

We don't track you across other sites, so we don't respond to browser Do Not Track signals. There is no tracking for them to switch off. The same goes for Global Privacy Control: we don't sell or share personal information for advertising, so there's nothing for the signal to opt you out of.

Security

Each account's data is kept apart from every other account's by rules in the database. Passwords are stored hashed. Access tokens for connected systems, and any Anthropic API key you store, are encrypted with a key that is held outside the database and is separate for each account. All connections use HTTPS. You can switch on two-step sign-in with an authenticator app.

The Security page has the full list, including what we do not have yet. No system is perfectly secure, and we can't promise that ours is.

If a security incident affects your data, we will tell you without undue delay, and as the law requires, including Utah law (Utah Code § 13-44).

Keeping and deleting data

We keep your account data while your account is open.

  • Deleting your account. You can delete your account yourself, under Security and your data. That deletes the account and everything in it.
  • Asking us. You can also email alex@steadyworkhq.com and ask us to delete data. We do it within 30 days.
  • Disconnected systems. Stored copies of data read from a system are deleted at once when you disconnect it. Drafts made from that data are removed within 30 days.
  • If a paid plan ends. If your plan ends and you keep your account, your automations and website chat are kept as they were, but they stop running until you are on a plan again.
  • Backups. Backups are kept by our hosting provider on its own schedule, and they expire on that schedule. Deleted data can remain in a backup until that backup expires.

We may keep billing records where the law requires us to. We keep email you send us as long as we need it to handle your request and for our records.

Your choices

  • You can download your data: your details, your interviews, your instructions and rules, your drafts, the names of your connections and reports, and the action log.
  • You can delete your account, and everything in it, yourself.
  • You can disconnect a connected system at any time.
  • You can switch two-step sign-in on or off.
  • You can remove your own Anthropic API key.
  • You can ask what we hold about you.
  • You can ask us to delete your correspondence.

The first five are in your account. For anything else, or if you can't find an option, email alex@steadyworkhq.com. We'll handle it.

Missed-Call Rescue

Missed-Call Rescue is a separate text-back service. It uses Twilio to send and receive text messages, so callers' phone numbers and the messages pass through Twilio. The service has its own terms inside the app.

Children

The app and this site aren't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version here with a new date. If you have an account and the change matters to you, we will also email you.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email alex@steadyworkhq.com.
