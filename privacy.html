Who we are

Steadywork is a sole-proprietor business in Ogden, Utah. This policy covers the Steadywork app and this website, steadyworkhq.com. You can reach us at alex@steadyworkhq.com.

What the app collects

  • Account details. Your email address, your password, and any name or business name you give us.
  • Your interview. What you tell the AI in the interview chat, the write-up made from it, and your ranked list of tasks.
  • Files you send in. Files you upload, and files you email to the private address we give you.
  • Data from connected systems. If you connect QuickBooks, Microsoft 365 (Outlook), Google (Gmail and Sheets), or Smartsheet, the app reads the data an automation needs from that system.
  • Automations and drafts. The instructions you give, the drafts the app prepares, what you approve, and a log of each action.
  • Connection details. The access tokens that let the app reach a system you connected. If you choose to use your own Anthropic API key, we store that key too.
  • Billing details. Your plan, your credit use, and your payment history. Payments are handled by Stripe.
  • Technical data. Standard server logs, such as IP addresses and the time of a request.
  • Messages to us. Anything you send us by email.

What this website collects

Almost nothing. The website is a static set of pages, separate from the app.

Server logs. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub, Inc. serves the pages and may log standard technical data like IP addresses, under its own privacy policy. We don't see or manage those logs.

Bookings. If you book a call, that happens through cal.com, a scheduling service we don't run. Whatever you enter there, such as your name, email, and notes, goes to cal.com and then to our calendar and email.

Email. If you email alex@steadyworkhq.com, we receive that email.

The website sets no cookies and runs no analytics. The calculator on the home-services page keeps nothing. It does math on the numbers you enter.

What we do with it

We use your data to:

  • run the interview, the ranked list, and your automations;
  • carry out what you approve, such as sending an email from your mailbox;
  • keep your account working, including sign-in and password reset;
  • bill you;
  • give you support, and prepare an audit or report if you ask for one;
  • reply to you and schedule calls;
  • keep the app secure and fix problems.

We never sell your information, and we never share it for marketing.

AI processing

The AI work in the app is done by Anthropic, through its Claude API. To produce an interview, a ranked list, or a draft, the app sends the content it needs to Anthropic. That can include what you said in the interview, the contents of your files, and data read from a connected system.

Under Anthropic's commercial terms, data sent through its API is not used to train its models.

Some automations upload a file to Anthropic for a run. Those files are deleted after the run.

If you use your own Anthropic API key, the same content is sent to Anthropic under your key. Your agreement with Anthropic then applies to that use.

Some automations run as fixed steps or a fixed program, with no AI. Each draft is labelled so you can tell which kind made it.

Companies that handle data for us

These companies handle data so that we can run the app. Each has its own privacy policy. We share your data with no one else, unless the law requires it.

Anthropic

AI processing. Receives the content needed to produce your results.

Render

Hosts the app and its database, in the United States.

Stripe

Handles payments. Your card details go to Stripe, not to us.

GitHub Pages hosts this website. cal.com handles call bookings. See the GitHub privacy statement and the cal.com privacy policy.

Connected systems

Connecting a system is optional. You can connect QuickBooks, Microsoft 365 (Outlook), Google (Gmail and Sheets), or Smartsheet. You sign in on that company's own page, so we never see your password for it.

A connection is read-only unless you grant a permission that lets the app write, such as sending email from your mailbox. Even then, nothing is sent or changed unless you approve that specific draft, or you have set that automation to act without waiting for approval.

Access tokens are stored encrypted, with a key that is separate for each account. You can disconnect a system at any time. After that, the app can no longer reach it.

Google accounts

If you connect a Google account, the app asks only for what the feature you are using needs, one permission at a time:

  • your email address, to show which account is connected;
  • permission to send email from your Gmail address, used only for emails you have approved, or that an automation you have set to act without approval sends;
  • permission to read, or to add a row to, a Google Sheet you name.

The app cannot read your email, file drafts in Gmail, or see your other files.

Steadywork's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • We use Google user data only to provide the features you see in the app.
  • To prepare a draft, the rows an automation reads from your sheet are sent to Anthropic, which processes them to produce the result. We do this only for that purpose and with your agreement, which the app asks for before you connect.
  • We do not use Google user data, and we do not let anyone else use it, to develop, improve or train general AI or machine-learning models.
  • We do not sell it or use it for advertising.
  • People at Steadywork do not read it, unless you ask us for help with it, it is needed for security, or the law requires it.

You can disconnect Google at any time under Connections. We then delete the access we hold and cancel it with Google. You can also delete your whole account, and everything in it, under Security and your data.

Teams

If you join a team with a team code, the team owner can read your interview write-up and the team's ranked list. You are told this when you join. If you don't want the owner to read something, don't put it in the interview.

Who at Steadywork can see your data

Steadywork may look at your account data to give you support. If you ask us for an audit or a report, we may look at it to prepare that. We don't look at it for any other reason.

Other people's information in your data

Your files and systems may hold information about other people, such as your customers' names, email addresses, and what they owe. We handle that information only to run the app for you, on your instructions. You are responsible for having the right to use it this way. If one of those people contacts us about their information, we will pass the request to you.

Cookies

The app sets one cookie, called sw_session. It keeps you signed in. The app cannot work without it. There are no advertising cookies and no tracking cookies.

This website sets no cookies.

Selling, tracking and opt-out signals

We don't sell personal data. We don't use ad tech or data brokers.

We don't track you across other sites, so we don't respond to browser Do Not Track signals. There is no tracking for them to switch off. The same goes for Global Privacy Control: we don't sell or share personal information for advertising, so there's nothing for the signal to opt you out of.

Security

Each account's data is kept apart from every other account's. Access tokens for connected systems, and any Anthropic API key you store, are encrypted. No system is perfectly secure, and we can't promise that ours is.

If a data breach ever affects your information, we'll notify you as Utah law requires (Utah Code § 13-44).

Keeping and deleting data

We keep your account data while your account is open. After you cancel, we delete your account and data when you ask us to. Email alex@steadyworkhq.com to ask.

We may keep billing records where the law requires us to. We keep email you send us as long as we need it to handle your request and for our records.

Your choices

  • You can disconnect a connected system at any time.
  • You can remove your own Anthropic API key.
  • You can ask what we hold about you.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and data, or your correspondence.

For any of these, email alex@steadyworkhq.com. We'll handle it.

Missed-Call Rescue

Missed-Call Rescue is a separate text-back service. It uses Twilio to send and receive text messages, so callers' phone numbers and the messages pass through Twilio. The service has its own terms inside the app.

Children

The app and this site aren't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version here with a new date. If you have an account and the change matters to you, we will also email you.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email alex@steadyworkhq.com.
