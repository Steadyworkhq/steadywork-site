Who we are

Steadywork is a sole-proprietor business in Ogden, Utah. This policy covers the Steadywork app and this website, steadyworkhq.com. The app is offered only to businesses located in the United States, and information is processed in the United States. You can reach us at alex@steadyworkhq.com.

What the app collects

  • Account details. Your email address, your sign-in details, and any name or business name you give us.
  • Your interview. What you type in the interview chat, the write-up made from it, and your ranked list of tasks.
  • Files you send in. Files you upload, and files you email in.
  • Data from connected systems. If you connect a system, such as QuickBooks Online, Microsoft 365 (Outlook), Google (Gmail, Sheets and Calendar), or Smartsheet, the app reads data from it for your automations. The app also stores the access tokens for that connection, and your own AI key if you choose to add one.
  • Automations and drafts. The instructions and rules you give, the drafts the app prepares, what you approve, the figures shown on your dashboard, and a log of actions.
  • Website chat. If you put our chat on your website: the facts you write for it, your visitors' messages, and any contact details a visitor gives. See Website chat.
  • Payment. Your plan, your credit use, and your payment history. Payments are handled by our payment processor, Stripe. Card details go to Stripe, not to Steadywork.
  • Technical data. Standard server logs, such as IP addresses and the time of a request, and a record of sign-ins and similar account events.
  • Messages to us. Anything you send us by email.

What this website collects

The website is separate from the app. It is served by a hosting provider, which may log standard technical data such as IP addresses under its own privacy policy. If you book a call, you do so through a scheduling service, and what you enter there is sent to us. If you email us, we receive that email. If you join the waitlist on the website, the email address you enter is sent to the app and kept so we can tell you when it opens.

How we use it

We use information to provide, maintain, secure and support the service. That includes running your interview, ranked list, automations, website chat and dashboard; carrying out what you approve or automate; billing; and replying to you.

The Google and QuickBooks sections below apply to data from those systems.

Who we share it with

We share information with service providers that help us run the service:

  • Our AI provider, Anthropic. To produce an interview, a ranked list, a draft or a chat answer, the app sends the content it needs to Anthropic. That can include what you typed in the interview, the contents of your files, data read from a connected system, and messages from visitors to your website chat. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, this content is not used to train its models. If you use your own Anthropic API key, the content is sent under your key, and your own agreement with Anthropic applies.
  • Our payment processor, Stripe. Card details go to Stripe, not to Steadywork.
  • Hosting providers, which host the app, its database and this website.
  • Email delivery providers, which deliver email the app sends and receive files you email in.
  • A text messaging provider, used for the separate Missed-Call Rescue service.

We also disclose information when the law requires it, and may transfer it as part of a sale or transfer of the business.

Systems you choose to connect, such as QuickBooks or Google, are your own providers. When the app sends an email or makes an entry that you approved or automated, that information goes to the system you connected.

The same applies to any other tool you tell the app to send results to, such as a Slack or Teams channel or an automation service like Zapier or Make. What you confirm is sent to the address you gave us, and from there that tool's own privacy policy applies. We store the address in encrypted form. If you have another tool send data into the app, we store what it sends as part of your account data.

Apps you connect from the Apps page work the same way. You sign in on that app's own page, and we store the access it gives us in encrypted form. We ask each app for permission to read only, unless you choose to allow changes. When an automation looks something up in the app, the answer is used to prepare your draft and is sent to our AI provider like the rest of your data. A change is made in the app only when you confirm that exact change. You can disconnect an app at any time.

Connected systems

Connecting a system is optional. You sign in on that company's own page, so Steadywork does not receive your password for it. You choose which permissions to grant.

You can disconnect a system at any time from within the app.

Google accounts

If you connect a Google account, the app asks only for what the feature you are using needs, one permission at a time:

  • your email address, to show which account is connected;
  • permission to send email from your Gmail address, used only for emails you have approved, or that an automation you have set to act without approval sends;
  • permission to read, or to add a row to, a Google Sheet you name.
  • permission to read the events on your calendar for the next seven days, used only if you choose your calendar as something an automation reads.

The app cannot read your email, file drafts in Gmail, or see your other files.

Steadywork's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • We use Google user data only to provide the features you see in the app.
  • To prepare a draft, the rows an automation reads from your sheet are sent to Anthropic, which processes them to produce the result. We do this only for that purpose and with your agreement, which the app asks for before you connect.
  • We do not use Google user data, and we do not let anyone else use it, to develop, improve or train general AI or machine-learning models.
  • We do not sell it or use it for advertising.
  • People at Steadywork do not read it, unless you ask us for help with it, it is needed for security, or the law requires it.

You can disconnect Google at any time under Connections. We then delete the access we hold and cancel it with Google. You can also delete your whole account, and everything in it, under Security and your data.

QuickBooks

If you connect QuickBooks Online (Intuit), the app reads data for your automations and can prepare entries, such as a supplier bill. For QuickBooks data:

  • We use it only to provide the features you set up.
  • To prepare a draft, it is sent to our AI provider, Anthropic, which processes it to produce the result.
  • We do not use it, and we do not permit any third party to use it, to train or develop AI models.
  • We do not sell it.
  • We delete it within 30 days after you disconnect QuickBooks or close your account, subject to record-keeping required by law.

Website chat

A business customer can put a Steadywork chat on its own website. The chat answers visitors automatically. The business decides to use the chat and is responsible for it, including for telling its visitors how their information is used.

Visitors' messages, and any contact details they give, are stored so the business can read them and follow up. Visitors' messages are sent to our AI provider, Anthropic, to produce the answer. Steadywork handles this information on the business's behalf.

If you are a visitor. If you used a chat on a business's website, contact that business about your information.

Teams

If you join a team, the team owner can read your interview write-up, the team's ranked list, and the team's automations and their dashboard. The team owner can assign your automation to another member to cover, or transfer it to another member. That member then sees its rules, drafts and history. What a cover person approves can go out through your mailbox or theirs, as the team owner chooses.

Access by Steadywork personnel

Steadywork personnel may access customer information as needed to provide, support, secure and improve the service, to comply with law, and to enforce the terms. For Google user data, the limits in the Google section apply. For QuickBooks data, the limits in the QuickBooks section apply.

Other people's information in your data

Your files and systems may hold information about other people, such as your customers. We handle that information on your behalf, to provide the service to you. You are responsible for having the right to use it this way. If one of those people uses the opt-out link in a marketing email you sent through the app, we keep their email address on your do-not-email list so that their choice is honoured. People whose information is in a customer's data should contact that customer.

Cookies

The app sets two cookies. Both are strictly necessary. sw_session keeps you signed in. sw_step is used during sign-in.

There are no advertising cookies and no analytics cookies. This website does not set cookies.

Selling, advertising and opt-out signals

We do not sell personal information. We do not share it for advertising.

The service does not track you across other sites and does not respond to browser Do Not Track signals. Because we do not sell personal information or share it for advertising, a Global Privacy Control signal does not change how the service handles it.

Security

Steadywork uses reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

If a security incident affects personal information, affected people will be notified as required by applicable law.

Keeping information

Information is kept as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law, and copies may remain in backups for a period. The QuickBooks and Google sections say more about data from those systems.

Your choices

  • You can download your data from within the app.
  • You can delete your account from within the app.
  • You can disconnect a connected system at any time.

Other requests about your information, including deletion requests, can be emailed to alex@steadyworkhq.com and will be handled as the law requires.

Missed-Call Rescue

Missed-Call Rescue is a separate text-back service. It uses a text messaging provider to send and receive text messages, so callers' phone numbers and the messages pass through that provider. The service has its own terms inside the app.

Mobile phone numbers and text-message consent collected for that service are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Children

The app and this site aren't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page and the date at the top will be changed.

Contact
