Can AI send emails for me?

Yes. The better question is whether you want it to press Send without you, and for most business email the answer is "not yet".

Checked 2026-11-18. Menus in other products change, so a label may differ slightly on your screen.

Short answer: an AI assistant can write emails for you today, and with a connection to your mailbox it can send them too. The safe way to use it is to have it prepare the emails and for you to approve them, at least until you trust it with that particular job.

Three levels of "AI sends my email"

  1. It writes, you send. You ask a chat assistant for a draft and paste it into your email. This works everywhere and needs no set-up. It saves the writing time and none of the admin.
  2. It writes into your mailbox, you send. The assistant is connected to your email and leaves finished drafts in your Drafts folder, addressed and ready. You open, check and send.
  3. It sends. The assistant sends from your address without you looking at each one.

Most of the value is at level 2. The email is written, addressed and has the right attachment. All that is left is a look and a click.

What can go wrong at level 3

  • The wrong person. Two customers with similar names, or an old address on file.
  • The wrong fact. AI can state an amount or a date with confidence and be wrong, especially if it has to work it out.
  • The wrong moment. A reminder sent an hour after the customer paid, or to someone you are in a dispute with.
  • Too many. A mistake in the instruction can be repeated to everyone on the list before you notice.

None of these are reasons to avoid AI. They are reasons to keep a person in the loop until a job has gone right many times.

A safe way to start

  1. Pick one kind of email you send over and over, such as payment reminders or appointment confirmations.
  2. Have the AI prepare them and review every one for a few weeks.
  3. Notice what you keep correcting and turn each correction into a rule: "never chase this customer", "always include the invoice number".
  4. Only when you have stopped making corrections, consider letting that one job send on its own.

Things to check in any tool

  • Does it send from your own address, so replies come back to you?
  • Can you see and edit every email before it goes?
  • Does it keep a record of what was sent, to whom and when?
  • Can you stop everything with one switch?
  • If you send marketing email, does it handle unsubscribes? The rules for that are yours to follow, whoever writes the email.
