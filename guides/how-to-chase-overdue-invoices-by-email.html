How to chase overdue invoices by email

Chasing money is awkward, so it gets put off, and the longer an invoice sits the less likely it is to be paid. A fixed schedule and ready wording take the awkwardness out of it.

Checked 2026-11-16. Menus in other products change, so a label may differ slightly on your screen.

Short answer: send a friendly reminder a few days after the due date, a firmer one about two weeks later, and a final notice at around 30 days. Each one names the invoice number, the amount and the due date, attaches the invoice again, and says exactly how to pay.

What every reminder needs

  • The invoice number, the amount owed and the original due date.
  • The invoice attached again, so they do not have to hunt for it.
  • One clear way to pay: a pay link, or your bank details.
  • A named person to reply to if there is a problem.

Most late payment is forgetfulness or a lost invoice, not refusal. Write the first reminder as if that is the case, because it usually is.

A simple schedule

  1. 3 to 7 days late: a friendly nudge.
  2. About 14 days late: a firmer reminder that asks for a payment date.
  3. About 30 days late: a final notice that says what happens next.

After that, pick up the phone. A call often settles in two minutes what three emails could not.

Wording you can copy

1. Friendly nudge

Subject: Invoice [number] - quick reminder Hi [name], Just a quick note that invoice [number] for [amount] was due on [date]. I've attached a copy in case it went astray. You can pay here: [pay link] If it's already on its way, thank you, and please ignore this. [Your name]

2. Firmer reminder

Subject: Invoice [number] is now [days] days overdue Hi [name], Invoice [number] for [amount] was due on [date] and we haven't received payment yet. A copy is attached. Could you let me know when we can expect it? If there's a problem with the invoice, tell me and I'll sort it out. You can pay here: [pay link] [Your name]

3. Final notice

Subject: Final notice: invoice [number] Hi [name], Invoice [number] for [amount] is now [days] days overdue, and I haven't had a reply to my earlier reminders. Please pay by [date]. If we don't hear from you by then, we will [pause further work / add the late fee in our terms / pass this on for collection]. If something has gone wrong on your side, call me on [phone] and we can agree a plan. [Your name]

Only state a consequence you are entitled to and willing to carry out. Late fees and collection rules differ by place and by contract, so check your own terms first.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Chasing someone who has paid. Check for payments that arrived that morning before you send.
  • Chasing a customer mid-dispute or on a payment plan. Keep a short list of people who are never sent the standard reminder.
  • Sending two reminders in the same week because two people in the office both thought it was their job.
