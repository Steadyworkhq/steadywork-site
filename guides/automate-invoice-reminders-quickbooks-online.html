How to automate invoice reminders in QuickBooks Online

QuickBooks Online has built-in reminders that are worth turning on. They also have limits that catch people out. Here are both.

Checked 2026-11-18. Menus in other products change, so a label may differ slightly on your screen.

Short answer: go to Settings, then Account and settings, then Sales. In the Reminders section, turn on Automatic invoice reminders and choose when each one is sent. QuickBooks then emails the customer for you.

Turn on automatic reminders

  1. Select the Settings gear, then Account and settings.
  2. Open the Sales tab. In some regions it is called Sales and Get Paid.
  3. Find the Reminders section and select Edit.
  4. Turn on Automatic invoice reminders.
  5. For Reminder 1, choose how many days before or after the due date it should go, and edit the wording.
  6. Add a second and third reminder if you want them, then save.

QuickBooks checks due dates a few times a day and sends a reminder when an unpaid invoice matches your rule. Invoices that have been reminded show "Reminded" in the status column.

What the built-in reminders cannot do

  • They cannot skip a customer. Intuit's help page says you cannot exclude specific customers. The customer you have a payment plan with, and the one in a dispute, get the same reminder as everyone else.
  • Everyone gets the same words. A customer who is three days late for the first time and one who is ninety days late for the fifth time read the same message.
  • Only invoices you emailed through QuickBooks are reminded. If you sent the invoice another way, no reminder goes out.
  • They come from QuickBooks, not from you. They do not appear in your own mailbox, and you do not see them before they go.
  • Up to three reminders, within 90 days either side of the due date.

When to stay with the built-in reminders

If you have a small number of customers, all on the same terms, and you are happy for every one of them to get the same note, turn the built-in reminders on and stop there. They are free and they work.

If you find yourself thinking "but not that customer", or you want to read each reminder before it goes, you need something that prepares the reminders and lets you approve them.
