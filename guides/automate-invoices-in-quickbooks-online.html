How to automate invoices in QuickBooks Online

If you bill the same customers the same amounts on a schedule, QuickBooks Online can create those invoices for you. Here is how, and where it stops being enough.

Checked 2026-11-18. Menus in other products change, so a label may differ slightly on your screen.

Short answer: use a recurring template. Open an invoice, choose Manage, then Scheduling, and turn on Make invoice recurring. Or go to Settings, then Recurring transactions, and create a new template. Choose Scheduled to have QuickBooks create the invoice for you on the dates you set.

The three kinds of recurring template

  • Scheduled: QuickBooks creates the invoice on the schedule you set. You can have it created a few days ahead so there is time to look at it.
  • Reminder: QuickBooks reminds you that the invoice is due to be created. You create it.
  • Unscheduled: a saved template with no dates. You use it when you need it.

Set up a recurring invoice

  1. Open an invoice for the customer, or start a new one with the usual lines on it.
  2. Choose Manage, then Scheduling, and turn on Make invoice recurring. On some screens this is a Make recurring button at the bottom.
  3. Give the template a name you will recognise, such as the customer and "monthly".
  4. Choose the type: Scheduled, Reminder or Unscheduled.
  5. Set how often, the start date, and how many days in advance to create it.
  6. Save the template. You can pause, change or delete it later under Recurring transactions.

Check how your version handles sending. In some regions Intuit's help says each recurring invoice is saved for you to review and send yourself.

Where recurring invoices stop being enough

  • The amount changes each time. A recurring template repeats the same lines. Hours worked, materials used or a meter reading have to be typed in by someone.
  • The invoice depends on something outside QuickBooks. A finished job on a schedule sheet, a signed-off timesheet or a delivery note is not something a template can see.
  • You want to check them before they go. Many offices review every invoice. A schedule that sends on its own works against that.
  • You want them sent from your own mailbox. See our guide on emailing QuickBooks Online invoices from Outlook.

If your billing is the same every month, recurring templates are all you need. If it varies, the work is in getting the right figures onto the invoice, and that is where help is worth having.
