Can Smartsheet send automated emails?

Yes, and it is one of the most useful things in Smartsheet. It also has limits that matter once the email is going to a customer.

Checked 2026-11-18. Menus in other products change, so a label may differ slightly on your screen.

Short answer: yes. In a sheet, open the Automation menu and create a workflow. A workflow can send an alert, a reminder, a request for an update or a request for approval when a row changes or a date arrives.

What Smartsheet can send on its own

  • Alerts: an email when a row is added or a column changes, for example when Status becomes "Complete".
  • Reminders: an email a set number of days before or after a date in the sheet.
  • Update requests: an email asking someone to fill in certain columns of a row, with a form to do it.
  • Approval requests: an email with Approve and Decline buttons, which writes the answer back to the sheet.

Set one up

  1. Open the sheet and choose Automation, then Create from template, or create a workflow from scratch.
  2. Choose what starts it: a row being added or changed, or a date being reached.
  3. Add conditions if you need them, such as "only when Assigned To is not blank".
  4. Choose who receives it. This can be named people or the contact in a column of that row.
  5. Write the message and choose which columns to include, then save.

What these emails cannot do

  • They come from Smartsheet, not from your mailbox. They look like system notices, they are not in your Sent Items, and replies do not thread with your other email.
  • Sending outside your team depends on permissions. Smartsheet says you can send to anyone with an email address, depending on your automation permission settings. If recipients are skipped, check those settings on both the sheet and the account.
  • The wording is the same for every row. You can drop column values into the message, but it cannot adjust its tone or leave out a line that does not apply.
  • It works one sheet at a time. A message that needs a figure from your accounts or another system cannot get it.
  • Nobody reads it before it goes. If the row is wrong, the email is wrong.

Which to use

For internal notices, such as "this job is ready for you", Smartsheet's own workflows are the right tool. For emails to customers, where the wording and the sender matter, you will want something that writes a proper email and lets you check it.
